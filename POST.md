# English

Introducing SubClock: a tiny offline SRT timing checker and precise subtitle shifter.

Review overlaps, invalid durations, duplicate cue numbers and reading-speed estimates. Export a standalone HTML report or shift captions by an explicit millisecond offset into a new file.

Zero third-party dependencies. Includes synthetic Arabic/English captions and 20 passing tests. No audio uploads, guessed alignment or automatic text rewriting.
